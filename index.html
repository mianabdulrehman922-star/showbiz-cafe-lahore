A Sophisticated Culinary Haven in Johar Town, Lahore

Nestled along Khayaban-e-Firdousi in Phase 2 Johar Town, Showbiz Cafe offers a cozy yet refined environment tailored for memorable catch-ups, relaxed family dinners, and peaceful coffee breaks.

Whether you are craving rich gourmet burgers, sizzling BBQ, velvety Alfredo pasta, or handcrafted cold shakes, our menu brings together verified crowd favorites crafted with high-quality ingredients and attentive hospitality.
